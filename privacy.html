privacy policy

last updated: june 2026

kittygotchi is a free Chrome extension. this privacy policy explains what data we do and don't collect.

data we collect

kittygotchi does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. all data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API and never leaves your browser.

what gets stored locally

  • your cat's happiness level
  • the last time you fed your cat
  • whether the cat is shown or hidden
  • whether you have dismissed the support banner

this data exists only on your device and is never sent to any server.

permissions we use

  • storage — to save your cat's state locally on your device
  • tabs — to trigger the feeding animation on the active tab when you click "feed a fish"
  • idle — to detect inactivity so your cat can fall asleep
  • host permissions — to display the cat companion on any webpage you visit

third party services

the extension contains a link to ko-fi.com for voluntary donations. visiting ko-fi is subject to their own privacy policy. no data is shared with ko-fi automatically.
